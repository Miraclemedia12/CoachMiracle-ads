Refund Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to any paid products, digital resources, or programs that may be offered by SkillForge Community in the future or currently.

1. Free Community Access

Access to the SkillForge community group/chat is free. There is no charge to join the discussion space. Therefore, no refunds apply to free community membership.

2. Paid Digital Products or Programs (If Offered)

If we offer any paid digital products, courses, templates, or coaching programs, the following general rules apply unless a specific product page states otherwise:

  • Digital products (downloadable files, templates, recorded content): Due to the nature of digital goods, all sales are generally final once access or download has been provided. Exceptions may be made on a case-by-case basis if the product is defective or not as described.
  • Live programs or memberships: Refund eligibility (if any) will be clearly stated on the sales page for that specific offer. Typical windows, if offered, are limited (e.g., 7–14 days) and require that significant content has not been consumed.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for a paid product (where eligible):

  1. Contact us through the method provided on the product purchase confirmation or the main website.
  2. Include your order details, email used for purchase, and reason for the request.
  3. We will review the request and respond within a reasonable timeframe (usually 5–7 business days).

4. Non-Refundable Items

  • Free community access
  • Products or services explicitly marked as non-refundable
  • Services already fully delivered
  • Cases of violation of our Terms & Conditions

5. Chargebacks

We encourage you to contact us first to resolve any issue. Unjustified chargebacks may result in restriction from future access to our community or products.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The version posted on this page with the latest “Last updated” date is the current policy.

7. Contact

For refund-related questions, please use the contact method available on the main website or within the community group.

Note: This policy is designed to be fair and transparent. SkillForge is primarily an educational community; most current offerings are free to access.
