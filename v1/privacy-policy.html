Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

SkillForge Community (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or join our community group.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Information you provide: If you contact us or join a community chat, you may share a name, username, or message content according to the platform’s (e.g. WhatsApp, Telegram) own rules.
  • Technical data: Basic device and browser information, IP address (anonymized where possible), and pages visited on this website for performance and security purposes.
  • Cookies / similar technologies: We may use essential cookies for site functionality. We do not use invasive tracking for advertising without consent where required by law.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve this website and the community experience
  • To respond to inquiries
  • To maintain security and prevent abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Community Groups

When you join a third-party group chat (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.), that platform’s own privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control how those platforms process your data. Please review their policies before joining.

4. Data Sharing

We may share limited information with service providers who help us host the website (e.g. hosting, analytics) under strict confidentiality. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law. Community chat history is governed by the third-party platform.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please reach out via the community group or the contact method provided on the main website.
